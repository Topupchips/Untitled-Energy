Questions& answers

01What makes it different from other energy drinks?

Less sugar, real fruit juice, and caffeine from green coffee beans rather than synthetic powder. It tastes like a sparkling fruit drink first.

02How much caffeine is in a can?

80 mg per 250 ml can, about the same as a single espresso. Most energy drinks sit between 150 and 200 mg.

03Is it sparkling?

Lightly. Closer to sparkling water than to cola, so it stays easy to drink ice cold.

04How much sugar and how many calories?

Between 3 and 4 g of sugar and 20 to 30 kcal per can depending on the flavour. The full panel is printed on the back of every can.

05Are there artificial sweeteners?

No. Sweetness comes from the fruit, rounded off with stevia leaf extract.

06Who shouldn't drink it?

Not recommended for children, during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or for anyone sensitive to caffeine.

07Where can I buy it?

Order cases of 12 or 24 here, mixed or single flavour. Orders over 48 cans ship free.
